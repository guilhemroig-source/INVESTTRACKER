Donald J. Trump

President of the United States · Executive Office of the President

Trump reported 3,884 sales and 6,212 purchases across 10,096 transactions on periodic transaction reports between January 2025 and July 31, 2026, disclosed in large batches. 7,398 of those transactions, or 73 percent, were flagged as filed late, meaning the filings certify the trades were reported more than 30 days after notification of them was received. The most recent filing, signed September 8 and posted by OGE on September 22, 2026, added 1,156 trades dated July 1 to July 31, 2026: 716 sales and 440 purchases, with 260 flagged as filed late. The filings concentrate in March 2026, which accounts for 2,349 transactions; the two busiest single days are May 15 and May 18, 2026, with 374 and 362 trades. A separate 21,115 rows come from the 2026 annual report and are labeled as such on this page.
